## What it is

One command, `npx agent-badges`, turns your work with Claude Code and Codex into an Apple Watch–style wall of 3D achievement badges. Everything runs on your machine: it reads only timestamps and usage counts from your logs, never your conversations, and uploads nothing.

## How it's made

Every badge is generated with three.js from a small config: enamel, polished and brushed metal, ceramic, holographic foil, crystal. Drag to spin one, or flip it over to read the engraving on the back.

There are 62 badges so far: 32 for Claude Code and 30 for Codex, covering milestones, streaks, features to discover, holidays and a few hidden ones. Tiered badges level up from bronze to silver to gold.

## What's fun about it

- Your year becomes a midnight-enamel plate with one sunken dot per day, brighter the more you used.
- A drawer under the heatmap holds twelve 3D month medals; the rim metal depends on how each month compares with your busiest one.
- Any badge exports as a 2048 px PNG, the whole wall becomes a share poster, and an achievement card can go on your GitHub profile.
