## What it is

MapPostcard AI: move the map anywhere in the world, it works out where you are through reverse geocoding, and AI paints a postcard of the place — an illustrated front with a matching decorative back.

## How it's made

- Images come from Google's Nano Banana models, three tiers trading speed for quality.
- Six art styles — watercolor, vintage, ink sketch, oil paint, ancient ink, cyberpunk — and seven aspect ratios.
- Token usage and an estimated cost are shown after each generation.

## What's fun about it

- Upload a photo of yourself and the AI redraws you into the scene in the postcard's style.
- The compass button picks a random city and style when you don't know where to go.
- Postcards collect in a floating ring binder (kept for the current session only).
